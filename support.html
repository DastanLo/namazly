The times do not match my mosque

Different mosques follow different calculation methods. Open Settings → Method and pick the one your mosque follows (Namazly preselects the method of your country). Then use Settings → Adjust times to shift any prayer by a few minutes. If your mosque’s timetable differs by more than a few minutes after that, email us the timetable and we will check the method.

Prayer alerts do not arrive

  • Allow notifications for Namazly in iOS Settings → Notifications. Turn on Time Sensitive so alerts can break through Focus modes.
  • Check that the prayer is not set to “Off” in Settings → Alerts.
  • iOS keeps at most 64 pending notifications, so Namazly schedules about 12 days ahead. Open the app once in a while so it can schedule further.
  • Silent mode and Focus can mute sounds; a banner will still appear.

The Qibla arrow is off

Move your iPhone in a figure 8 away from metal and magnets to calibrate the compass, and make sure location access is on. The accuracy notice in the app tells you when calibration is needed.

Namazly Plus and subscriptions

  • Prayer times, alerts, Qibla and the monthly timetable are free. Plus adds statistics and the qada counter.
  • Restore: Settings → Restore purchases (also on the Plus screen). Use the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Namazly Plus → Cancel. Or open the Plus screen in the app and tap Manage subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

My data

Everything (location, prayer log, qada counts, settings) is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account to delete: deleting the app removes it. See the Privacy Policy.
